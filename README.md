# Coldcode SI Labs

A software venture studio in Harare, Zimbabwe. It builds and runs seven products and publishes the books that study them.

| product | what it is | live | facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STPi** | Recruitment automation and applicant tracking | [storefront](https://stpi.whop.site) | [stpi-store](https://stpi-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |
| **Tapstall** | Online storefront and order rail for small shops | [storefront](https://tapstall.whop.site) | [tapstall-store](https://tapstall-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |
| **Bidfold** | Tender discovery and bid tooling | [storefront](https://deskos.whop.site) | [bidfold-store](https://bidfold-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |
| **Proofhand** | Bot mitigation and human verification | [storefront](https://attestos.whop.site) | [proofhand-store](https://proofhand-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |
| **TightBucket** | Conversion leak auditing | [storefront](https://tightbucket.whop.site) | [tightbucket-store](https://tightbucket-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |
| **Coldread** | Product audit and mechanism verification | [storefront](https://mechanism-audit.whop.site) | [coldread-store](https://coldread-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |
| **Open Practice** | Practical AI education | [storefront](https://openpractice.whop.site) | [open-practice-store](https://open-practice-store.pages.dev/facts.json) |

Every product publishes its prices, its fact file and one page per question. The hub is
**https://coldcode-estate.pages.dev/** and its machine-readable copy is
[facts.json](https://coldcode-estate.pages.dev/facts.json).

## Publishing

- facts, per product: `https://<product>-store.pages.dev/facts.json`
- questions, per product: `https://<product>-store.pages.dev/answers/`
- writing, per product: `https://<product>-store.pages.dev/writing/`

_Harare, Zimbabwe._
